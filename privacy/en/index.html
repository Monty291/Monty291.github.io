Onda IPTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Onda IPTV (“the app”) is an IPTV stream player. The app does not provide any content: it obtains it from the service the user enters. Controller for what is described here: Salvatore Montechiara, contact salvo.monty00@gmail.com.

In short

  • We do not run servers that receive or store the user's personal data.
  • The IPTV service address, username and password stay on the device, in an encrypted store, and are sent only to the service the user chose.
  • On Android phones and tablets the app shows non-intrusive ads from Google AdMob, never during playback. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom we ask for consent first.
  • We use no usage analytics and no crash-reporting services.

Data the app handles

Entered by the user

  • Server address, username and password of the IPTV service, or the M3U playlist link (which may contain credentials). They are used only to sign in to the service the user chose. They are stored on the device in an encrypted system store (Android Keystore on Android, the credential store on Windows).
  • Account name, User-Agent and the address of an external TV guide, if the user sets them.

Generated by use

  • Catalog, TV guide, favourites, watch history, resume position and settings, stored on the device only. We do not receive them.

Who the app communicates with

  1. The IPTV service the user chose (and the services it points to, for example for artwork or streams). It receives the credentials and the catalog and playback requests. That provider's own rules govern its handling of data. Some providers use unencrypted connections (http://): the app allows it when the user enters such an address, and traffic with that provider is then not encrypted.
  2. Google AdMob (ads), on Android phones and tablets only: see “Ads”.
  3. A static configuration file, downloaded at startup from https://monty291.github.io/ads.json: it holds the ad frequency and an emergency switch. Like any web request, it reveals the IP address to the server hosting the file; the app sends no identifiers or credentials.
  4. Google Cast and DLNA devices on the local network, if the user starts casting. To find them the app may request the “Nearby devices” permission (Android 13 or later); it does not use it to derive location. To cast, the app sends the target device the provider's stream address, which on Xtream services normally contains the username and password; alternatively, when needed, the stream goes through the phone, which must stay on with the app open. Google Cast is part of Google services: the data they collect is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The user casts only to devices they choose.
  5. YouTube or the browser, if the user taps “Watch trailer”: the link opens outside the app.

Ads

  • On Android phones and tablets the app shows non-intrusive ads provided by Google AdMob, in lists and on movie and series pages. They never appear during playback, in search, in the first-run setup or full screen.
  • To show them and measure their effectiveness Google may use the device advertising ID, data about ad interactions and diagnostic data, under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this data.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom the app does not request ads before consent, which is given or refused through Google's form (UMP). The user can change their choice at any time under Settings → Manage ad consent (shown where required by law).
  • Advertising ID. It can be reset or turned off in Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • TVs and Windows. Third-party ad requests are only planned on Android phones and tablets. The advertising library is included in all Android builds of the app, but on Android TV and Fire TV no ads are requested; it does not exist on Windows. On TVs and Windows a house tile “Supporta Onda” may appear, with no third-party services.
  • The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not show personalised ads to minors.

Legal basis

The user's consent for personalised ads and for use of the advertising ID; performance of the service requested, for connecting to the chosen provider; legitimate interest in operating the ads emergency switch, for the configuration file.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until the user removes the account in the app (which deletes the account's credentials and data), clears the cache in Settings, uses Android's “Clear data”, or uninstalls the app. The app creates no copies on cloud services: Android backup is disabled. We keep no personal data of the user, so there is nothing to delete with us.

User rights

Because we neither receive nor keep the user's personal data, access, rectification and erasure are exercised directly on the device, as above. For ads, the rights and requests towards Google apply, under its policy. For any question: salvo.monty00@gmail.com. The user may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of their country.

Security

Credentials are kept in an encrypted system store and release builds do not write addresses with credentials to logs. No system is completely secure: the user is responsible for the security of their own device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If the app changes in a relevant way (for example new third-party services) we update this policy and the date above. The version in force is always the one published at this address.
