Onda IPTV

Live TV, movies and series in a single app

The IPTV player for Xtream Codes services and M3U playlists, made for Android phones and tablets: a grid TV guide, multiple accounts and a full-featured player.

Get it on Google Play How to use

Android 7.0 or later

Important: Onda IPTV does not provide any content, channels or playlists. You need an IPTV service or a playlist from a provider of your choice, which you must have the right to use.

See it in action

What you can do

  • Live TV with a guide

    Grid TV guide (EPG) and details for every programme.

  • Movies and series

    Plot, seasons and episodes; “Continue watching” picks up where you left off.

  • Catch-up

    Watch past programmes, when your provider offers the archive.

  • Search everything

    Channels, movies and series in a single search.

  • Multiple accounts

    Switch in one tap. Favourites and watched markers for each.

  • Full player

    Audio and subtitle selection, subtitle styling, picture-in-picture and mini-player.

  • Cast to your TV

    Google Cast (Chromecast) and DLNA TVs, from an Android phone.

  • English and Italian

    With a language switch in Settings.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and choose Xtream Codes or an M3U playlist.
  2. Enter the server address, username and password of your service, or the link to your playlist.
  3. Wait for the first sync: live TV, movies and series are ready.

Your privacy

The server address and credentials stay on your device, in an encrypted store, and are sent only to your provider. No account to create with us, no usage analytics, no cloud backup. Read the privacy policy.

Free, with non-intrusive ads

Onda IPTV is free and complete. It shows non-intrusive ads, never during playback and never full screen; in the European Economic Area it asks for your consent first.

Frequently asked questions

Does Onda IPTV provide channels or content?
No. Onda IPTV is only a player: you need an IPTV service or an M3U playlist from a provider of your choice, which you must have the right to use.
Which services does Onda IPTV work with?
Services that offer Xtream Codes access (server address, username and password) and M3U playlists.
Is Onda IPTV free?
Yes, it is free and complete. It shows non-intrusive ads, never during playback and never full screen; in the European Economic Area it asks for your consent first.
Where are my login details stored?
Only on your device, in an encrypted store, and they are sent only to your provider. No account is needed with us, and there is no usage analytics or cloud backup.
Which devices can I use it on?
Android phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or later. From an Android phone you can cast to Google Cast (Chromecast) devices and DLNA TVs.
Does it have a TV guide (EPG)?
Yes: a grid programme guide and, when your provider offers the archive, the option to watch past programmes (catch-up).
