How to use Onda IPTV

Onda IPTV is a player for IPTV services. The app does not provide any content, channels or playlists: you need an IPTV service (Xtream Codes access) or an M3U playlist from a provider of your choice, which you must have the right to use.

What you need

  • Xtream Codes: the server address (for example http://server.com:8080), the username and the password your provider gave you.
  • M3U playlist: the playlist link your provider gave you.

Add an Xtream Codes service

  1. At first launch tap Get started; later, open Accounts and tap Add account.
  2. Choose Xtream.
  3. Enter Server, Username and Password. The Name is optional: it only helps you recognise the account if you have more than one.
  4. Tap Sign in.

Add an M3U playlist

  1. Open Add account and choose M3U playlist.
  2. Paste the playlist address.
  3. Tap Add playlist.

M3U playlists contain live channels: the Movies and TV series sections only appear with an Xtream service that offers them.

The first sync

After signing in, the app downloads the catalogue and the programme guide: the first time it can take a while, depending on the size of the service. When it finishes, a short three-screen tour shows the sections and the controls. You can replay it any time from Settings → Help & feedback → Replay the tour.

Using the app

  • Sections: For you, Live TV, TV guide, Movies and TV series.
  • Quick actions (add to favourites, mark as watched and more): press and hold a title or a channel. On Windows you can also right-click; on a TV, hold OK or press Menu.
  • Favourites: from the quick actions; you find them in the ★ Favorites category of each section.
  • Multiple accounts: from Manage accounts you add, edit or delete accounts; Switch account moves between them.
  • Casting: on phones and tablets you can send the video to a Google Cast (Chromecast) device or a DLNA TV on your network.
  • Language and startup: in Settings choose the language, the section that opens at startup and whether to resume the last channel.

If something does not work

  • “Invalid server address” or “Invalid playlist address”: check that it starts with http:// or https:// and that there are no extra spaces.
  • “The address is not an M3U playlist”: the link does not lead to a playlist, or it has expired: ask your provider for an updated link.
  • Account expired or inactive: this comes from your provider; the app cannot renew it.
  • No programme guide, or wrong times: open Edit on the account and check TV guide source and TV guide time correction.
  • The video does not start: some providers limit simultaneous connections; stop playback on other devices and try again.

Legitimate use and privacy

Only use services and content you have the right to watch. Onda IPTV has no affiliation with any content provider. The server address and credentials stay on your device, in an encrypted store, and are sent only to your provider. All the details are in the privacy policy.
